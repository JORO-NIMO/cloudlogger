Welcome

See what is happening on your website.

CloudLogger keeps a simple diary of what happens on your website. You can see when someone places an order, signs up, or runs into a problem, all in one place. There is nothing complicated to learn. Add your website and we take care of the rest.

How it works

Three easy steps

First, create an account and add your website. Next, show us that the website is really yours by adding a short piece of text that only the owner can add. Then share a few lines with the person who looks after your website. From that moment, everything that happens appears on your page, newest first.

Privacy and ownership

Your visitors stay private. Your information stays yours.

When your website tells us who did something, we scramble the person's name and internet address before saving anything. The scrambled version cannot be read or turned back into the original. We only record activity for websites that have been proven to belong to you. Nobody else can open your pages.
